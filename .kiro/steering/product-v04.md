# The Bureau's Logic (v0.4) — project steering

The game's horror is bureaucratic reasoning: change a premise and an authoritative room must change. Build one compelling physical cause/effect loop rather than adding decorative AI, a new framework, services, or accounts. Browser-only, original art, zero runtime dependencies.

Keep two validated states distinct: GOAL (logical proof exists) versus RELEASED (player successfully submitted). A contradiction disables release, not further editing. Preserve all 30 finite configurations with tests. Protect versions and never claim Kiro usage without genuine Kiro execution evidence.
